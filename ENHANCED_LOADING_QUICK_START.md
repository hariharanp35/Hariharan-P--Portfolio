## 🎯 **Features Summary**

### **Rings Animation** 🔄

- Inner ring: 8-second rotation (clockwise)
- Outer ring: 12-second rotation (counter-clockwise)
- Semi-transparent green and blue
- Creates sense of motion and sophistication

### **Floating Shapes** 🌀

- 4 geometric shapes floating randomly
- Full 360° rotation on each shape
- Up/down and left/right floating motion
- Semi-transparent (15-35% opacity)
- Positioned at strategic corners

### **Corner Glows** ✨

- Top-left: Green (4-second pulse)
- Top-right: Blue (5-second pulse)
- Bottom-left: Green (5.5-second pulse)
- Adds depth and frames the design
- Premium, subtle effect

### **Progress Counter** 📊

- Real-time 0-100% display
- Monospace font (technical look)
- Updates smoothly with progress bar
- Clear feedback to users

### **Shimmer Effect** ✨

- Animated shine across progress bar
- 1.5-second animation cycle
- Creates sense of activity
- Modern UI pattern

### **Glowing Dots** ⚪

- Expands from 1.0 to 1.3 scale
- Green box-shadow that pulses outward
- Staggered timing (0, 0.2s, 0.4s)
- More impressive than plain dots

### **Tagline** 💬

- Text: "CRAFTING DIGITAL EXPERIENCES"
- Color pulses between gray and bright green
- Inspirational, professional message
- Context and meaning to loading

### **Status Text** 📝

- "Initializing Portfolio..." message
- Opacity fade pulse animation
- Subtle confirmation of progress
- Appears after other elements

### **Breathing Background** 🌌

- Opacity animation (0.3 → 0.5)
- Scale animation (1.0 → 1.1)
- 6-second continuous cycle
- Adds life to background
